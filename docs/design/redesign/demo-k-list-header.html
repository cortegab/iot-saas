Demo K: list page headers

Your Paint sketch of the Devices page, weighed against today's list layout (DESIGN.md §6), and three ways to build on it. The search and the status figures, segments and tabs filter the list. Every option keeps the table, the footer and pager, the peek and the empty, loading and error states as they are. Only what sits above the table changes.

Your sketch

The direction is right: the table starts sooner, and the page reads as one band of controls above the list.

What it gets right

  • The description goes beside the title. That saves a line and loses nothing.
  • KPIs fit on one line. The numbers are what matter. The explanation lines were most of the strip's height.
  • The primary button sits with the controls. The top of the page becomes one band instead of three.

What I'd reconsider

  • The KPI strip is already a status filter. Clicking a figure filters the list. As its own card it still costs about 60 px, and only 2 of the 7 lists have one, so the lists don't share one layout. B and C fold it into the filter.
  • A button in the toolbar needs a second place. The toolbar isn't shown while the list loads, fails or is empty, so the button has to jump back to the header then. C avoids that.
  • The short explanations ("waiting for first message") become hover text, which touch screens don't show.
  • The stray marks by the numbers, the arrow on the left of "Last seen" and the cut-off table bottom are Paint artefacts. They're left out here.
