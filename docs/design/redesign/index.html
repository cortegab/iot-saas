Demo H: Guided

A clickable prototype of the whole iodriven app, built to work at laptop and phone widths. To create something, you go step by step; to edit, you use short tabs; one item at a time opens in a side panel. It keeps DESIGN.md's colours, fonts and status words. Nothing is saved, and the readings are simulated.

The bar at the top of the demo sets the frame width (1440, 1366, 1280, 1150, 820, 390), switches light and dark, and simulates a lost live connection.

What's in it

Rules: Form

  • The rule as one sentence on top; each phrase opens a small editor (a bottom sheet on phones)
  • New rule: recipes, then a 5-step wizard; edit: tabs When · If · Then · Behaviour · Activity
  • Conditions and actions as one-line rows; each opens in a side panel. All/Any groups, nested
  • Hold time, minimum interval and clear delay always visible; Re-arm vs Latch as illustrated cards
  • One check: a red count blocks saving, an amber "!" warns and never blocks
  • Preview beside the editor on wide screens (24 h replay, Would send, Checks, conflicts, dry run); a Preview button elsewhere

Rules: Ladder (expert)

  • The same rule and the same side panels as the Form
  • Horizontal when it fits; a vertical rung on narrow screens, with no sideways scrolling
  • Draws what today's ladder leaves out: the clear rung, clear-delay and hold timers, the interval, and latch with its reset
  • Every element can be focused; the arrow keys move between them and Enter opens one

Notifications → alarms

  • Active, Acknowledged and Cleared, with repeats grouped (×4)
  • Acknowledge with a note, one or many; everyone sees who and when
  • A timeline for each alarm; severity, device and rule filters; the bell counts active alarms only

Dashboards, Controls, Connect

  • Dashboard edit: click a widget to change its type, source, title and size; add one in three steps; a reorderable list on phones
  • Controls: requested vs reported, a 30 s countdown, then "No response" with Retry; a command timeline
  • Connect: a step-by-step wizard. The one-time password survives a reload, and leaving before you've stored it asks first

From the first round

  • Icon rail on laptops, full sidebar from 1440, bottom tab bar on phones
  • Templates and Add device: a wizard to create, tabs to edit, a side panel for each part, metric or actuator
  • Lists fold columns as space shrinks and become cards on phones; filters move into a panel
  • 24-hour chart axis with the time zone named; a reconnecting banner; skip link; arrow keys in tabs and menus

  • §7 editors: a wizard for creating; one item at a time in a side panel

Rebuilding

The demo is generated from src/: shared base.css, data.js, core-*.js, plus h.css, h.js, h-rules.js and h-extras.js. Run bash docs/design/redesign/src/build.sh after editing. Demos I and J (the other two directions) were explored and not kept.

This is a design prototype. DESIGN.md adopted it on 2026-10-20; the platform's code follows PR by PR (DESIGN.md §14).
