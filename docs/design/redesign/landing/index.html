Landing page demos

Three takes on the iodriven.tech landing page, written for readers halfway between non-technical and technical: plain sentences first, with technical detail one step away. They use DESIGN.md's tokens, fonts and status words. Each one uses Framer Motion (framer-motion 14.14.0, loaded from esm.sh) and still reads fine without it. Use the chip at the bottom right to switch between light and dark.

The demos

A linear walk-through: what it does, how it works, why it's safe, who it's for.

  • Hero: a rule's whole life replayed on a loop
  • A "Technical note" under each step
  • Who it's for: site teams, integrators, developers
  • Animated scroll reveals

Recommendation: a section for integrators

Yes, add one. The product already has what integrators need (a workspace per client, Simple view, scene buttons, remote device health, three ways to host), but the current landing page never says who it's for. The audience is also different: the integrator chooses the platform and the client lives with it. Each demo shows a different size of section:

Demo 1: one section of six cards · Demo 2: a full section with an interactive Full/Simple preview and a project timeline · Demo 3: one compact night band.

If integrators become the main sales channel, give them their own page (/integrators) and keep a band on the landing page that links to it.

Other new sections

  • Use cases (demos 1 and 3): concrete settings help non-technical readers see themselves.
  • Who it's for (demo 1), For developers (demo 2): moves the MQTT contract out of the FAQ.
  • Interactive safety (demo 3): shows why hold time and hysteresis matter instead of explaining it.

Prototype notes: copy is English only, the forms are not wired up, and "Recommended section" in demo 1 is a review annotation, not page copy. Demo 1 and its sign-in page are built (DESIGN.md §10, 2026-11-27): the landing page and every account page follow them. Demos 2 and 3 are kept as alternatives.
