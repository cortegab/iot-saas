How it works

Every rule is a sentence you can read out loud.

Whoever you are, the core is the same: devices send readings, rules decide, outputs move, and you get told. The words in a rule are the settings.

When temperature on Greenhouse bay 1 is above 30 °C for 10 s, turn Fan 1 on and notify Night shift. Turn it back off below 28 °C, and act at most once a minute.

Readings arrive

Any sensor that reports a number or a yes/no: temperature, level, pressure, a door, a motor's current.

Rules decide right away

Checked the moment a reading lands, not every few minutes. Across one device or a whole site.

Safely, every time

Hold time, a reset margin and a minimum interval are part of every rule, so noisy sensors don't wear out relays.

For developers

A small contract, kept stable.

Build your own hardware or connect your own software. Everything a device needs fits in a few MQTT topics and two small JSON messages.

  • MQTT over TLS, one credential per device, which can only touch its own topics.
  • Commands that expire. Each carries a time to live, and the intended state is retained, so a device that reconnects catches up instead of replaying old orders.
  • REST API with API keys and role limits, described by an OpenAPI schema.
  • Webhooks as a rule action, to hand events to your own systems.
  • Ready-made ESP32 firmware with health reporting and signed over-the-air updates, if you'd rather not write your own.

FAQ

Short answers.

Can my clients break something?
Viewers can't change devices, rules or settings, whatever view they use. Simple view hides and rewords things; the permissions behind it are enforced by the server.
Do I pay per client?
Pricing depends on how many devices you run and where. Tell us about your projects and we'll suggest a setup.
What hardware do I need?
Supported ESP32 boards run ready-made firmware flashed from the browser. Anything else can speak MQTT directly or join through an edge connector.
What if a site loses its internet connection?
Devices that are offline can't take commands. When they reconnect they get the latest intended state, and stale commands expire instead of running late.
Is there a phone app?
Yes, for Android first: dashboards, controls, alerts with push notifications, and Bluetooth Wi-Fi setup for new boards.
